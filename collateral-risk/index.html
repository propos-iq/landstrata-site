PropOS IQ resolves that title and reads the proprietor's live corporate status at the point of underwriting instead.

Architecture

AI At The Interface.
Determinism At The Decision.

PropOS IQ uses AI where language and retrieval are the hard problem, and deterministic rules where the answer has to be defensible. A lender can ask a question in plain English and get a readable case file — but no risk finding in that file originates from a model.

WHAT THE AI DOES

Resolve, Retrieve, Explain

Interprets the request, resolves it to a property or UPRN, orchestrates retrieval across the underlying datasets, and writes the case file in language an underwriter can read. It handles the interface, not the judgement.

WHAT THE AI NEVER DOES

Decide The Risk

Every risk finding comes from a versioned deterministic rule carrying its statutory authority, jurisdiction and the date the law was assessed at. No finding is generated, inferred or softened by a language model.

THE VALUATION MODEL

Machine Learning, Stated Plainly

The market value is produced by a gradient-boosted model trained on 30M+ Land Registry transactions, validated out-of-time at 9.6% median absolute percentage error, with a confidence grade attached to every property rather than a single headline accuracy claim.

WHY IT MATTERS

Reproducible By Design

The same property assessed against the same rule version returns the same finding, with the evidence retained. That is what makes the output reviewable by a credit risk or model governance function rather than merely persuasive.

Governance position: statutory findings are rule-based, versioned and evidenced; valuation is a statistical model with published out-of-time performance and per-property confidence. The two are reported separately and never blended into a single unexplainable score.

Built for the standard, not around it. The 2025 RICS Red Book permits AI and automated models in valuation where there is appropriate governance, human oversight and professional judgement — and PS 1 records the IVS 105 position that no model, an AVM included, produces an IVS-compliant valuation without a valuer applying that judgement. PropOS IQ is designed for the valuer to apply it: assessed, evidenced and traceable, presented for professional judgement rather than in place of it.
Mortgage Property Risk

Common Questions.

A concise explanation of where PropOS IQ sits in a mortgage lender's property-risk and valuation workflow.

What is residential collateral risk?

It is the property-specific risk that matters when a home is being relied on as security for a mortgage. PropOS IQ focuses on statutory, environmental, ownership and location-specific evidence such as coal mining, flood, ground stability, contamination, planning context and the status of the registered proprietor.

Does PropOS IQ replace an AVM?

No. PropOS IQ is designed to sit alongside an existing AVM or valuation. The valuation answers what the property is worth; PropOS IQ provides an independent evidence-backed view of relevant property risk beneath that value.

How is coal mining risk handled?

Where Coal Authority evidence indicates a relevant coalfield risk, PropOS IQ records the evidence and can recommend the appropriate next step, such as a CON29M coal mining search before reliance.

Why does the freeholder's status matter?

On a leasehold property, the freeholder is responsible for management, service charge collection and lease enforcement. Where the registered proprietor is in administration, has a receiver appointed or is heading for strike-off, that responsibility is compromised — a fact recorded on the public register but rarely surfaced at the point of underwriting.

Why assess risk at UPRN level?

A UPRN provides a consistent property reference for joining evidence from multiple datasets to the same location, helping keep the assessment traceable and reproducible.

Is PropOS IQ an AI product?

Yes — AI-native by design, and deliberately bounded. AI resolves the property, retrieves evidence across the underlying datasets and writes the underwriter note. The risk findings themselves come from versioned deterministic rules, each carrying its statutory authority and the date the law was assessed at, so the same property returns the same finding every time. The market value is produced by a gradient-boosted statistical model with published out-of-time performance and a confidence grade per property. That split is not a compromise: the 2025 Red Book permits AI in valuation with governance and human oversight, and requires a valuer's professional judgement for an IVS-compliant valuation. PropOS IQ is built to be the evidence a valuer applies judgement to.

Is statutory risk priced into the value?

No. Statutory risk is assessed, flagged and evidenced with full provenance. PropOS IQ reflects a risk in the value only where the market robustly evidences a discount, rather than applying an adjustment the evidence does not support.
